Why Walkthrough
See it run

Put work on a track. Watch it finish.

ToteBoard is the Agentic TODO Board for VS Code. You drop an item on Slow or Fast. Agents expand, plan, and build. You keep the gate.

SLOW you press play FAST auto-runs Expand → Plan → Build Lives in todo/ in the repo
Tracks

Two speeds, one board

Slow is gated — items wait for a play or a next-step flag. Fast is auto-run. Both default to Expand, Plan, Build, then move to Complete.

Entry

Quick entry, then close

Title in, Save and Close. Stay in the tree. Edit later if you need a brief. The board is for capture, not ceremony.

Control

Play when you mean it

On Slow, status flips to ready and a play button appears. Nothing starts until you say go — or you right-click Expand Now.

Signal

Spinners you can trust

E, P, and B icons spin while the agent works, then go solid. When the required steps finish, the item lands in Complete.

Views

Tree, Kanban, archive

Work the sidebar. Flip to Kanban when you want columns. Open the HTML archive viewer when you want a standalone read of the board.

Agents

The extension owns the queue

OpenCode or Copilot runs Expand / Plan / Build. MCP can add items. Skills stay mini so the board — not chat — drives the work.
