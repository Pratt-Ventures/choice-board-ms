How this was calculated
Power Choice converts your pairwise answers into Bayesian win-rate estimates. New comparisons are selected to improve sparse, uncertain, or unpredictable areas. Weighted results use your factor comparisons to infer relative importance; unanswered cells receive hierarchical prior estimates rather than being treated as zero.
