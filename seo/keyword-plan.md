## Research evidence

- [Atlassian’s decision-making process guide](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/teamwork/decision-making-process)
  presents a sequence from identifying the decision through evaluating outcomes.
  Inference: the process query needs an actionable workflow. ChoiceBoard’s proposed
  contribution is the team-specific treatment of roles, independent input, and dissent.
- [Smartsheet’s decision matrix templates](https://www.smartsheet.com/decision-matrix-templates)
  offers downloadable weighted and unweighted templates.
  Inference: a page targeting “decision matrix template” must supply an actual
  reusable template, with explanatory prose supporting it.
- [ASQ’s decision matrix guidance](https://asq.org/quality-resources/decision-matrix)
  explains weighted criteria and warns about inconsistent scoring direction.
  Use as a primary methodological reference; do not assert that matrices inherently
  hide disagreement or that one method is universally superior.
- [1000minds’ pairwise comparison guide](https://www.1000minds.com/decision-making/pairwise-comparison)
  addresses definition, charts, comparisons, and scaling limitations.
  Inference: the guide must include both a concrete example and limitations.
  Vendor descriptions are evidence about that vendor, not independent proof of superiority.

Only the first three topics were researched in depth during the foundation pass.
Later targets remain seed-based priorities and need their own search and source
review before intake. Revisit query ownership using Search Console and Bing data
once access is available.
