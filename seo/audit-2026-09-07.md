## Interpretation of SEO and AI-search requirements

The site guide's title/description character limits and explicit crawler list
are editorial policy. They should not be reported as a guarantee of ranking.
[Google's AI-feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)
says normal SEO practices apply and special AI files or schema are not required.
The requested AI exports are provided as an additional access format, with no
claim that every assistant reads them. Accurate visible content, crawlability,
and consistent structured data remain the foundation.

[IndexNow's documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation) requires a
hosted verification key. An accepted submission means the notification was
received; it does not guarantee indexing. The helper enforces local indexed
status and a matching live key file before submission.

## Human publication checklist

Source every figure and comparison, verify credentials and quote permission,
read the live voice corpus, confirm full transcripts and chapters, inspect image
layout and alt text, check mobile and keyboard behavior, validate supported rich
results, verify the analytics property, and obtain the author's publication yes.
No local checker can replace these judgments or account-dependent checks.
