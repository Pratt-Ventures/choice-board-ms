## LinkedIn

Collecting everyone’s opinion is only part of a group decision.

The team also needs to know who makes the call.

Atlassian’s DACI framework assigns one Approver and records the evidence, options, decision, and rationale.

Our guide turns those pieces into a four-step process and shows where to preserve dissent.
