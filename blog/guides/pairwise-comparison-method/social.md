## X

Five options create 10 distinct pairs when every pair is compared once. This guide shows the arithmetic, the head-to-head process, and what to do when preferences form a cycle.

## LinkedIn

Pairwise comparison makes one judgment smaller: compare two options at a time.

A complete set for five options contains 10 distinct pairs.

Those judgments can still form a cycle: A over B, B over C, and C over A.

The guide shows how to run the exercise and inspect what the answers establish.
